01Artist collaboration

Movement × Adeel uz Zafar

A limited apparel drop built on Adeel uz Zafar's mummified-animal drawings, launched with Sanat Initiative.

Take the artist's work off the gallery wall without flattening it into a print. The bandaged figures stay monochrome and stay drawn — the garment becomes another surface they live on, not merchandise they decorate. The launch was staged inside the gallery so the canvases and the clothes read as one body of work.

The source drawing. Monochrome, bandaged, hand-rendered.

Artist films — the drawing being made, and Adeel on what the collaboration is for.

Product and packing films, cut for sound off.

02Culture-led collection

Movement × Lyari Drop

A capsule tied to a neighbourhood rather than a season.

Make it read as Lyari-made, not Lyari-themed. The print collages the place itself, so the garment carries its own location. Shot on the ground in black and white, local cast, no studio, no grade beyond contrast.

Back print — from Lyari to the world.

03Product art direction

Retro State — Game Over

Two tees relaunching a dormant label, with no media budget.

One remembered feeling per tee: Game Over for the hours that disappeared in front of a screen, Chaos for the plans that never went to plan. Product floated in an arcade-lit still life — CRT, cartridges, vinyl — with pixel artefacts as the only graphic device.

The Game Tee and the Chaos Tee.
